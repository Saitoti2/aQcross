# aQross Student Hub

MASTER BUILD PROMPT — aQross Shopping & Delivery Platform

1. PROJECT IDENTITY

Build a complete, production-quality aQross Shopping & Delivery Platform.

The exact product name is:

aQross

The capitalization is intentional and must be preserved everywhere.

This is a student-focused ecommerce and shopping delivery platform designed to allow students to discover, compare, purchase and receive everyday products from multiple participating shops.

The platform is essentially a multi-store ecommerce marketplace combined with local delivery.

The primary target users are students who need convenient access to everyday necessities without having to physically visit multiple shops.

The platform should accommodate everyday student needs including, but not limited to:

Cereals & Grains

Butchery

Sanitary Essentials

Stationery

Pharmaceuticals

Snacks & Drinks

Fruits & Vegetables

Personal Care

Household Essentials

Baby Care

Pet Supplies

Electronics

Sports & Fitness

and additional relevant ecommerce categories as the marketplace expands.

The system must support shopping by product/category as well as shopping by specific shop.

This is a fundamental part of the product architecture.
